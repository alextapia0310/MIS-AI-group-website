Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

The WannaCry ransomware attack began on May 12, 2017. This malware encrypted files on infected computers and demanded Bitcoin payments to restore access. It affected hundreds of thousands of computers in more than 150 countries, disrupting hospitals, businesses, and other organizations. The attack became a significant event in cybersecurity history because it exploited a Windows vulnerability and demonstrated the importance of security updates and regular data backups.
