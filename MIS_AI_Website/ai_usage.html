Correct AI Usage

AI should only be used for assignments when the teacher instructs it.
However, unless the instructor states otherwise, it is perfectly okay to use AI to study for classes.
AI can be an extremely useful tool when it comes to explaining topics not easily understood in class.
Correct AI use is only using it to enhance one's knowledge of a topic, not to cheat.

AI Alternative Perspective: While asking AI to explain complex topics is helpful, rely on it cautiously.
AI models can occasionally "hallucinate" or present incorrect information with confidence.
Cross-referencing AI explanations with your course textbook or primary source material is necessary to ensure accuracy.

    Benefits of AI!

  1. Helps to increase understanding of difficult topics.
  2. Can help instructors grade work faster.

    Who Benefits?

  1. Students who missed a class and need material explained to them.
  2. Teachers who have a large amount of work to grade and have limited TAs to help.

    How are AI Tools Used?

  • Revising a student essay and suggesting grammar edits.
  • Making flashcards to help with an upcoming exam.

Incorrect AI Usage

Nondiscolusure
This is when a student does not tell their professor that they used AI in their assingment, such as improper citing.
Plagiarism
A student stealing work produced by AI and turning it in while claiming it's their own work.
Fabrication
When AI makes up information, the student takes it as fact, and turns it in using incorrect or nonexisting sources.
I believe that AI is a wonderful tool for helping students and teachers alike in college settings. It can be used to help enhance student understanding of difficult topics and help instructors grade work in a timely manner. However, the issue comes when AI use isn't disclosed. Not specifying when AI is used can lead to disaster, such as consequences for academic dishonesty.
